# A Universe Full of Love — For Kirthikha 🌿

An exquisitely romantic, luxurious, cinematic, and emotionally powerful digital universe dedicated to Kirthikha ("Panniyaram").

> *"Even if the universe never ends, my love for you will always be greater. Against the entire world, I will choose you. In every lifetime, it will always be you and me."*

---

## 🌿 Design Language & Aesthetics

- **Luxury Emerald Forest Green & Warm Cream Palette**:
  - Forest Night Void: `#040B08`
  - Velvet Emerald: `#0E241B`
  - Pine Green: `#143327`
  - Soft Sage Eucalyptus: `#7A9A8B`, `#9EB8AB`
  - Misty Sage: `#C4D6CD`
  - Warm Cream Ivory: `#F4ECE1`
  - Milk Cream: `#FFFDF9`, `#FAF6EE`
  - Cream Parchment: `#F7F1E6`
- **Typography**:
  - Royal Editorial Serifs: *Cinzel*, *Playfair Display*, *Cormorant Garamond*
  - Intimate Handwritten Letters: *Alex Brush*, *Great Vibes*
  - Body Sans: *Plus Jakarta Sans*
- **Atmospheric Visuals**:
  - Interactive deep forest cosmos canvas with twinkling cream stars and drifting pine nebulae
  - Fluttering delicate cream jasmine petals and soft sage eucalyptus leaves
  - Interactive celestial cream/sage starlight cursor with trailing stardust (desktop)
  - Shimmering cream-sage thread of destiny connecting two orbiting souls
  - Interactive wax-sealed royal cream parchment letter with deep forest typography
  - Built-in Web Audio romantic piano & strings symphony

## 🌌 Chapter Journey

1. **Prologue — Opening Experience**: Full-screen cinematic cosmic reveal: *"For the girl who became my entire universe..."* → *"Kirthikha 💚"* → *"Somewhere between billions of stars, my heart found you."* → Cream animated **ENTER MY UNIVERSE** button.
2. **Chapter I — Infinite Cosmos**: *"THE UNIVERSE MAY HAVE NO END. BUT MY LOVE FOR YOU IS GREATER."* with cosmic scale comparisons and a cinematic climax reveal: *"YOU ARE MY ENTIRE UNIVERSE."*
3. **Chapter II — Unshakable Devotion**: *"LET THE ENTIRE WORLD STAND AGAINST US. I'LL STILL CHOOSE YOU."*
4. **Chapter III — Tender Heart (Forgiveness)**: Intimate creamy ivory parchment letter with royal velvet emerald wax seal and deep forest cursive signature: *"Always your boy."*
5. **Chapter IV — Two Souls, One Destiny**: Two glowing stars slowly moving together, bound by a shimmering cream and sage thread of destiny.
6. **Chapter V — The Sacred Vow**: *"IN EVERY LIFETIME, I'LL FIND YOU."* Four solemn promises across life's journey.
7. **Chapter VI — Eternal Truth (Emotional Climax)**: Tiny glowing cream heart gradually unveiling: *"I LOVE YOU, KIRTHIKHA."* and a secret button *"ONE LAST THING..."* with celebratory cream and sage starlight confetti.

## 🛠️ How to Run Locally

To launch the development server and view the experience:

```bash
cmd /c npm run dev
```

Open your browser at `http://localhost:3000` to step into the universe!
